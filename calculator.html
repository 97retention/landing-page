How the math works

Churn compounds. Each month you lose a percentage of the clients you still have, so the loss shrinks a little as your book gets smaller. That's why 7.5% a month works out to about 61% a year, not 90%.

Clients lost per year = clients × (1 − (1 − monthly churn)12). Revenue walking out = clients lost per year × average monthly revenue × 12, the yearly value of the accounts you lose.
